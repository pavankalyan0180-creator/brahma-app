About Brahma

Brahma is your lifestyle guardian. It helps you move from unhealthy patterns to healthy routines by combining health profile data, daily check-ins, progress analytics, and practical guidance.

What Brahma Tracks

  • Body profile: age, height, weight, goal, activity, sleep, stress.
  • Daily check-ins: sleep, water, exercise, food quality, mood, and notes.
  • Lifestyle scores: nutrition, physical, sleep, stress, and habits.
  • Trend charts and guidance recommendations.

How to Use It Best

  • Fill your health profile fully and honestly.
  • Add a check-in every day, even if it is a rough estimate.
  • Review the stats page every 3 to 7 days.
  • Choose one improvement action each week and follow it consistently.
